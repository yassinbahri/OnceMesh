# Code of conduct

OnceMesh contributors must keep technical discussion respectful, specific, and
focused on observable behavior. Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate
misrepresentation of evidence, and publication of another person's private data
or credentials are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate this standard
and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Reports should be
sent privately to the maintainers through the hosting platform. Security issues
must follow `SECURITY.md`.

Enforcement decisions should identify the violated behavior, preserve reporter
privacy, avoid retaliation, and be documented to the extent safely possible.
